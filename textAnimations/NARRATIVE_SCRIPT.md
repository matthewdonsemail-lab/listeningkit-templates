# Narrative script: Log launch film

matt's voiceover, line by line, with every shot new. Nothing from Intro 1 is reused. The cyberpunk pre-comp we've
been building is the middle of the film: it already plays lines 5 to 10 in order. The rest is built the same way,
in the same world:
- **The opening:** a new scene before cyberpunk, in the same marble hall it opens on.
- **The ending:** new shots after it, carrying on from its last shot (the caricatures).

The method is the same as cyberpunk:
- generated stills locked to one composition;
- cut-outs on their own layers;
- glitch collage;
- cyberpsycho lines;
- the CRT over everything;
- every shot a pre-comp built from layers you can edit.

The captions are new cards in the same "going round the screen" look as Intro 1, two to four words a card, cut on
the spoken phrase. Times are estimates (about 2.3 words a second) until the VO is recorded.

## The story

1. **Before the split, lines 1-4 (new opening).** The marble hall from above, in warm morning light. AI arrives
   like light through the windows. Everyone slows down and looks up. Then a crack runs through the marble and splits
   the hall in two.
2. **The split, lines 5-10 (cyberpunk, as built).** The same hall gone cold. She walks in, and the crowd won't
   settle on one face. Her face won't settle on one style either. Hermes Agent can become her. Then the Nous logo,
   and she's someone different to everyone, down to the caricatures.
3. **Becoming you, lines 11-15 (new ending).** Her voice as a line that copies itself onto everyone she meets. The
   uncanny AI message falls apart, and hers holds. Back up over the hall: every person is a conversation, and the
   warm light comes back.
