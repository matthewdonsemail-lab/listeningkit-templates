# Additional Hermes typography

These are retained library experiments. Intro 1 now uses the animated product interfaces documented
in [UI_DEMOS.md](UI_DEMOS.md), replacing these three typography scenes and all blueSweep instances.

Generated by `hermesExplainers.mjs`; source commands and exported templates live in the matching
effects folders. All precomps are 1920 x 1080 at 30 fps, with editable text, native shapes and effects.

- askHermes (4.2 s): Tell Hermes / what you need. A prompt card reads, Read my DMs. Who needs a reply?
- socialActions (4.2 s): One agent. Three staggered cards: Read, Reply, Post. Across your connected accounts.
- yourControl (4.2 s): Your accounts. Your voice. Your call. Nothing sends until you approve.
- blueSweep (0.8 s): transparent transition precomp containing three moving diagonal blue panels.

Schoolbell highlights have blue fill, white/navy outlines and a slight tilt. Native character animators
and whole-text scale pops produce overshoot entrances. Card backgrounds lead their labels; scene
elements are staggered and shrink out before the end. The top Page push in adjustment layer applies
the existing subtle shared motion. Essential Graphics exposes all copy.

The final 0.22 seconds of each explanatory scene contain its shrink outro, timed underneath the blue
sweep so the outgoing copy holds its size until the transition covers it. The sweep's main colour is
editable in Essential Graphics; navy and light blue create the trailing edges.

Copy is grounded in log-cli's README: plain-language agent requests, reading/replying/posting with
connected accounts, voice matching, and dry-run behavior until live sending is requested.

Rendered full-resolution hold frames of the new scenes and the assembled sequence to check text fit,
holds and exits. See INTRO1.md for assembly timing, transitions and the saved-edit workflow.
