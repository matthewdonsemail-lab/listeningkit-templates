# Motion conventions

Use the platform reel (`type04`, generated by `iCanDo.mjs`) as the default pattern for scenes like this.
Keep every element editable and define its timing in the generator so rebuilding preserves the result.

## Stagger entrances

Stagger related elements instead of bringing them all in at once. For a logo card, the order is:

1. Rounded rectangle scales from zero, overshoots, then settles.
2. Logo scales from its centre with overshoot, starting two frames later.
3. Label starts another two frames later, typing on with character overshoot and a whole-label scale pop.

At 30 fps, two frames is 0.0667 seconds. Card entrance lasts 0.24 seconds; logo and label each take
0.25 seconds. Keep the platform rows staggered as well, with a clear pause at the heading before advancing.
Use the shared back easing (overshoot coefficient 1.8) to make the parts feel related.

For a heading such as "I work on", animate words with position, staggered by 0.1 seconds: enter from
above with overshoot (800 px, 0.38 seconds), hold, then anticipate and exit through the bottom
(800 px, 0.32 seconds). In type04 the exit starts at 2.2 seconds. Keep its resting size stable.

## Fit the container throughout the motion

Use rounded rectangles with 24 px corners, rather than capsule ends. Measure the completed label at
a settled time, never at the hidden first frame of a typewriter. Include the logo, the gap before the
label, outer padding, and the text's animated bounds and scale when sizing the rectangle. Check both
the resting frame and the largest overshoot frame. Text edits must continue to resize the rectangle.

## Add motion to the complete page

Put an adjustment layer named `Page push in` above all scene content. Apply the native Transform effect
to that layer so the background, heading, cards, logos and labels move together.

For this three-second scene, scale increases slowly from 100% to 108% and rotation from 0 to -1.2 degrees.
Anchor Point and Position are both [960, 540] in the 1920 x 1080 comp. Animate effect properties,
not the adjustment layer's own transform. Expressions use normalized comp time so changing the duration
preserves the full push. Keep the rotation subtle, and check that the push covers the rotated frame edges.

## Review and delivery

Render and inspect entrances, holds, overshoot peaks and exits. After changing the project, close
EffectCraft, save the updated project and export its template, then reopen EffectCraft on the changed
comp for visual review. Do not leave an old copy open alongside it. Commit and push only when requested.

The platform reel uses ListeningKit blue #2A8CFF, light blue #C9E3FF and navy #0D2A4C, over a warm white
#FFFEFC background with faint blue and peach dots fading toward the centre. Its platform labels are
Twitter/X, Facebook, Discord, Slack, Instagram, Reddit and LinkedIn. The logos come from the same
Font Awesome package used by log-cli, with their license vendored alongside the SVGs.
