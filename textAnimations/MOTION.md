# Motion conventions

For commentary-led exposition, use [EDITING_STYLE.md](EDITING_STYLE.md). Let a large, stable
typographic thought carry the narration, with words timed to the speech and a completed reading
hold. Short montage or concept beats bridge those statements. Keep exposition motion restrained;
use the large camera swings for the relevant visual insert. Prefer the concept and outcome when
a literal platform interface does not help explain the spoken thought.

For an orderly outward camera track, see [THREE_STEPS_TRACK.md](THREE_STEPS_TRACK.md) and
`threeStepsTrack.mjs`. Place complete 2D cards at alternating X positions, one eye level and
successively more negative Z depths. Keep physical camera Z retreating every frame; use native
camera Zoom for longer visual pushes, then yank wide before the next large pan. Connect the poses
with continuous velocity and retain the shared visible focus spring. Gate future panels and their
connections until the camera has passed their planes; a 3D stroke crossing the near plane can flare.
The current track uses a natural cubic camera curve with continuous acceleration, sparse poses
and a broad damped spring. Avoid stacking rapid lens pullbacks, roll reversals and scale rebounds
on each arrival: they feel stiff. Complete cards always exit by anticipating slightly larger and
scaling to zero; keep their opacity at 100%. The three-step scene uses brand-blue paths and an
oversized 3D instance of the existing dither background, behind the full track, for camera parallax.
Check background coverage through the widest pans and pullbacks; keep the source component unchanged.

Use the platform reel (`type04`, generated by `iCanDo.mjs`) as the default pattern for scenes like this.
Keep every element editable and define its timing in the generator so rebuilding preserves the result.

For native 3D product demonstrations, use the [EffectCraft motion skill](../skills/effectcraft-motion/SKILL.md).
Build the complete component in 2D, then place one instance in the camera scene at the required Z depth.
See [PLAY_JOURNEY.md](PLAY_JOURNEY.md) for lateral travel and [RESEARCH_DEPTH.md](RESEARCH_DEPTH.md)
for zoom-out/pan/zoom-in moves through information panels. Time whole-card scale peaks to camera arrivals.
The research camera connects anticipation, the wide pan, a rotating push-in and post-pan rebound
through one continuous-velocity path. All four focus bounces share one scale curve. Its white
connections draw toward the next panel and clear after arrival with Trim Paths End/Start.

## Stagger entrances

Stagger related elements instead of bringing them all in at once. For a logo card, the order is:

1. Rounded rectangle scales from zero, overshoots, then settles.
2. Logo scales from its centre with overshoot, starting two frames later.
3. Label starts another two frames later, typing on with character overshoot and a whole-label scale pop.

At 30 fps, two frames is 0.0667 seconds. Card entrance lasts 0.24 seconds; logo and label each take
0.25 seconds. Keep the platform rows staggered as well, with a clear pause at the heading before advancing.
Use the shared back easing (overshoot coefficient 1.8) to make the parts feel related.

For a heading such as "I work on", animate words with position, staggered by 0.1 seconds: enter from
above with overshoot (800 px, 0.38 seconds), hold, then anticipate and exit through the bottom
(800 px, 0.32 seconds). In type04 the exit starts at 2.2 seconds. Keep its resting size stable.

## Fit the container throughout the motion

Use rounded rectangles with 24 px corners, rather than capsule ends. Measure the completed label at
a settled time, never at the hidden first frame of a typewriter. Include the logo, the gap before the
label, outer padding, and the text's animated bounds and scale when sizing the rectangle. Check both
the resting frame and the largest overshoot frame. Text edits must continue to resize the rectangle.

## Add motion to the complete page

Put an adjustment layer named `Page push in` above all scene content. Apply the native Transform effect
to that layer so the background, heading, cards, logos and labels move together.

For this three-second scene, scale increases slowly from 100% to 108% and rotation from 0 to -1.2 degrees.
Anchor Point and Position are both [960, 540] in the 1920 x 1080 comp. Animate effect properties,
not the adjustment layer's own transform. Expressions use normalized comp time so changing the duration
preserves the full push. Keep the rotation subtle, and check that the push covers the rotated frame edges.

## Review and delivery

Keep product-play sequences quick and cut them to a beat grid. Use two beats per platform as the
default; the current journey uses 120 BPM, seven platforms in eight seconds. With a supplied music
track, align the grid to that track. Whole-card entrances overshoot, camera arrivals overshoot and
rebound, and a brief horizontal squeeze / vertical stretch releases into the resting proportions.
Maintain a small dolly or drift between hits. For the research scene, pull out before panning and
push in after the pan; keep the stages distinct. Use its smooth `brandBlueGradient` and white 3D
connecting lines. Speed up nested previews with the edit so their
masked reveals and typed actions complete during the short focus window. Use the built dither
gradient as a nested background; keep its original source controls and other scenes intact.

Render and inspect entrances, holds, overshoot peaks and exits. After changing the project, close
EffectCraft, save the updated project and export its template, then reopen EffectCraft on the changed
comp for visual review. Do not leave an old copy open alongside it. Commit and push only when requested.

The platform reel uses ListeningKit blue #2A8CFF, light blue #C9E3FF and navy #0D2A4C, over a warm white
#FFFEFC background with faint blue and peach dots fading toward the centre. Its platform labels are
Twitter/X, Facebook, Discord, Slack, Instagram, Reddit and LinkedIn. The logos come from the same
Font Awesome package used by log-cli, with their license vendored alongside the SVGs.
